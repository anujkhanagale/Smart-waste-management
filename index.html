How the system works

1

Find a SmartWaste station.

Locate a physically marked recycling point in your neighborhood or school campus.

2

Scan the QR code.

Use your mobile device to access the station entry and quick identification screen.

3

Login to your account.

Sign in using your mobile number and password, or use the admin account to review activity.

4

Select the waste category.

Choose one or more categories: Dry, Wet, E-Waste, or Other / Mixed.

5

Deposit waste into the correct bin.

Place each item carefully into the matching waste section to support sorting.

6

The smart scale measures the waste.

Every deposit is validated by a simulated measurement and a digital check.

7

Upload a photo as proof.

A picture helps confirm the item and waste category for the review dashboard.

8

Submit the weight.

Finish the form and let the platform auto-calculate your points for this deposit.

9

Receive 2.5 points per kg.

Your reward points are added instantly when the deposit is approved after review.

10

Redeem points for demo rewards.

Use your points to generate reward vouchers for your account balance.

Redeem 100 Points for ₹50 Amazon Reward Voucher?

By confirming, 100 points will be deducted from your balance and a reward code will be generated. This voucher is for the platform experience only and is not redeemable outside the app.
